\begin{abstract}
A technique for integrating expert military instruction with algorithmic strategy,
developed at the Human Adaptive Teaming Systems lab at the USC Institute for
Creative Technologies. Informed initialization discovered optimal strategies in
30\% of the compute time required by baseline algorithms.
\end{abstract}

\section{Motivation}

A reinforcement learning agent starting from a random initialization has to
rediscover, through millions of samples, things a domain expert could have simply
said out loud. In military planning domains that expertise already exists in
written doctrine and in the heads of the people the system is meant to assist.

The question is not whether to use that knowledge but how to inject it without
giving up the agent's ability to find strategies the experts did not anticipate.

\section{Method}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \resumeItem{Instruction as prior}{Expert instruction is encoded into the
  network's initialization, so search begins from a region of policy space that
  doctrine already endorses.}

  \item \resumeItem{Preserved exploration}{The prior shapes where search starts,
  not where it is allowed to go --- the agent remains free to depart from doctrine
  when the return justifies it.}
\end{itemize}

\section{Results}

Optimal strategies were discovered in roughly \textbf{30\% of the compute time} of
baseline algorithms trained from scratch.

\section{Context}

Conducted at the USC Institute for Creative Technologies, Human Adaptive Teaming
Systems lab, September 2023 -- May 2025.
