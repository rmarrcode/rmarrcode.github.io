About Me

Hi I'm Ryan. I'm a machine learning engineer and researcher. Welcome to my site.

Previously I worked with several labs at the University of Southern California. At USC ATRI I built the neuroimaging pipeline currently being used to study Alzhmeimer's Disease. I also worked with the USC HATS group on reinforcement learning. I focused on building systems that align human goals with AI systems.

Currently I work at Hidonix Industries as a machine learning research engineer.

In my spare time I do my own AI research. I use this site to share and document my projects. Most of my recent work involves verified AI, continual learning, and AI for science.
